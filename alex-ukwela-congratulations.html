Hon. Abraham Mora, Ibaji LGA Secretary, Congratulates Chairman-Elect Hon. Alex Ukwela

Abraham Mora Speaks | Ibaji, Kogi State

Hon. Abraham Mora congratulates Hon. Alex Ukwela.

IBAJI, KOGI STATE — Hon. Abraham Mora, Secretary of Ibaji Local Government Area, has congratulated Hon. Alex Ukwela on his emergence as Chairman-Elect of Ibaji Local Government Area, Kogi State.

In his congratulatory message, Hon. Mora extended his goodwill to Hon. Ukwela, expressing optimism about the prospects for unity, peace, and development under the incoming administration.

He emphasized the importance of cooperation among political leaders, community stakeholders, traditional institutions, and residents in promoting good governance and advancing the collective interests of the people of Ibaji.

Hon. Mora also expressed hope that the incoming administration would prioritize grassroots development, improve the welfare of residents, and promote initiatives that address the needs of communities across the local government area.

He noted that leadership comes with significant responsibility and called for unity, mutual understanding, and constructive collaboration among stakeholders to ensure continued progress in Ibaji.

The Ibaji Local Government Secretary encouraged the people to support peaceful coexistence and work together towards building a stronger, more united, and prosperous local government.

Congratulations to Hon. Alex Ukwela, Chairman-Elect of Ibaji Local Government Area.
