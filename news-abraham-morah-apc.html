Hon. Abraham Morah Urges Ibaji People to Remain Supportive of APC

Reported by Ibaji TV
Ibaji Local Government Area, Kogi State

IBAJI TV REPORT: Hon. Abraham Morah, Secretary of Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State, has called on the good people of Ibaji to remain united, peaceful and actively engaged in the political process.

In a message to the people of Ibaji, Hon. Abraham Morah urged residents and supporters of the All Progressives Congress (APC) to remain committed to the party and continue participating in its activities.

According to Hon. Abraham Morah, continued political participation and cooperation among the people are important to maintaining unity across the various communities that make up Ibaji Local Government Area.

He expressed confidence in the decision of those who have chosen to support the APC, stating that he believes they will have no reason to regret their continued support for the party.

Hon. Abraham Morah also appealed to the people of Ibaji to maintain peace and avoid actions or statements capable of creating unnecessary tension or division among residents.

He emphasized the importance of peaceful coexistence, respectful political engagement and cooperation among community leaders, youths, women and other stakeholders within the local government.

The Secretary encouraged residents to remain responsible in their political activities and to continue communicating with one another respectfully, irrespective of their political differences.

He further noted that the development of Ibaji requires collective participation and cooperation from its people, encouraging residents to remain focused on issues affecting their communities and the local government as a whole.

Hon. Abraham Morah concluded by appreciating the people of Ibaji for their continued support and called on residents to remain peaceful, united and committed to the development of their communities.
