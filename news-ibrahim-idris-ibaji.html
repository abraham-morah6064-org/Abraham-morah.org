A Story by the Ibaji Local Government Secretary

WHAT HIS EXCELLENCY ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS MEANT TO IBAJI

Tribute to His Excellency Alhaji Ibrahim Idris
His Excellency Alhaji Ibrahim Idris

For the people of Ibaji Local Government Area, the passing of His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, carries a particular weight. To many of us, he was not simply a former Governor of Kogi State; he was a leader whose name and years of service remain connected to the wider story of our people and our place within Kogi State.

Ibaji is a community with a rich history, resilient people, and a strong sense of identity. Over the years, our people have looked to leaders at the state level for recognition, inclusion, development, and a voice in the affairs of the state. The period of Alhaji Ibrahim Idris's leadership was therefore part of an important chapter in the political and developmental journey of Ibaji and Kogi State as a whole.

Personally, I will remember His Excellency as a leader whose administration represented a period in which the concerns of communities across Kogi State formed part of the larger conversation about governance and development. His years in office coincided with an important period in the evolution of our state, and Ibaji, like other communities, was part of that larger Kogi story.

For us in Ibaji, his passing is therefore more than the loss of a former Governor. It is the loss of a fatherly figure from an important generation of leadership. It is the closing of a chapter that many of us witnessed and experienced, and it gives us an opportunity to reflect on the relationship between leadership and the communities that leaders are called upon to serve.

I believe that one of the greatest responsibilities of leadership is to ensure that no community feels forgotten. The people of Ibaji have always valued leaders who recognise their presence, appreciate their contributions, and understand their aspirations. In remembering Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, I remember a period in our state's history when Ibaji was part of the broader journey of Kogi State under his leadership.

His legacy, like that of every public servant, will continue to be discussed and assessed through the experiences of the people and communities he served. But at this moment of mourning, my focus is not on politics. It is on humanity, remembrance, and respect for a life that was dedicated for many years to public service.

On behalf of the people of Ibaji Local Government Area, I extend our deepest sympathy to the Idris family. We mourn with you, we stand with you, and we pray with you.

May Almighty Allah reward His Excellency for every good thing he did for the people of Kogi State and for humanity. May Allah forgive his shortcomings, accept his good deeds, grant him Al-Jannatul Firdaus, and give his family the strength to bear this profound loss.

From Ibaji, we remember him. From Kogi, we mourn him. And as a people, we pray for the repose of his soul.

Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un.

May Almighty Allah grant him eternal rest and comfort the entire Idris family.

Signed:

Hon. Abraham Morah
Secretary, Ibaji Local Government Area
